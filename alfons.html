ALFONS Publisher

ALFONS Publisher is an internal desktop application used by the Světlonoši creator team to manage, schedule and publish short-form video content to social media platforms.

Purpose

The application supports the Světlonoši production workflow, including preparation of social media posts, publication scheduling, publishing and review of content performance.

TikTok integration

When a Světlonoši team member connects a TikTok account, ALFONS Publisher uses TikTok authorization only for the permissions explicitly approved by that user. The integration is intended for managing the team's own content and accounts.

Data and authorization

Authentication tokens and account connection data are stored locally on the authorized computer and are used only to provide the requested publishing and account-management functionality.
